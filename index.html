The Strategy

Real change requires honesty about what power actually does. A single MP cannot rewrite the Treasury's ledger overnight. However, an MP can relentlessly bring the hardest issues to the floor of the House until government ministers are forced to answer for them in public.

My promise to Oplana remains clear: I will put forward the legislation our constituency needs, argue for it transparently, and make sure you know exactly who stands in the way of progress. This is about pragmatic accountability.

Campaign Platform

Public Services and Fair Taxation

Our health system must serve every neighborhood efficiently. We are fighting for a national standard that mandates a dedicated GP-to-specialist referral desk in major constituency clinics like ours, with guaranteed, ring-fenced funding from the health ministry.

Additionally, fair taxation must be more than a slogan. We are actively pressing the Treasury to reform the property levy bracket to ensure commercial landholdings pay their fair share, and we stand firmly behind budgets that increase taxes on extreme wealth and luxury goods.

Affordable Housing Policy

Housing policy must reflect local realities. We are pushing for a national rent cap framework that ties allowable rent increases directly to Oplana's median income, rather than an inflated capital-city average.

To ensure this policy translates into actual homes for residents, we have drafted a vacancy disincentive clause. This will heavily tax residential units kept deliberately empty, guaranteeing that our housing supply remains active and available for those who need it.
