# Project Context: Adam Scholz for Oplana 2064

## Overview
This repository contains the source code for a website used in a political government simulator. The website is dedicated to the fictional 2064 Constituency election in Oplana, the Capital of Caprica.

## Candidate and Party
- **Candidate:** Adam Scholz
- **Party:** Social Democratic Alliance (SDA)

## Design System
- **Primary Color:** Rose Red

## Campaign Platform & Key Issues

### 1. Public Services and Fair Taxation
- **Healthcare Accessibility:** Proposing a national standard requiring a standing GP-to-specialist referral desk in any clinic serving a constituency above a defined population threshold (including Oplana). Funding to be ring-fenced in the health ministry's own appropriations.
- **Fair Taxation:** Calling on the Treasury to restructure the national property levy bracket (which currently undertaxes multi-unit and commercial-scale landholdings). Promising to vote for a budget that increases the Wealth and Luxury Goods Tax.

### 2. Affordable Housing Policy
- **Rent Cap:** Introducing a national framework rent cap, tying permissible rent increases on newly listed units to each constituency's own median income rather than a single capital-city benchmark.
- **Vacancy Disincentive:** Paired with the rent cap, introducing a clause taxing residential units left deliberately unlisted past a defined threshold to prevent landlords from pulling units off the market.

## Campaign Strategy
Adam Scholz is running on a platform of honesty and pragmatic legislation. He emphasizes that he does not control the budget or the treasury, but promises to introduce these bills, argue them honestly, force votes to get responses on the record, and expose which parties or MPs block progress.
